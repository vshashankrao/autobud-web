Common questions

How do I post a job?

From the Home tab, tap the "+" to post a job. Add a title, describe the problem, pick the vehicle, and submit. Nearby mechanics see your post and can send quotes.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, vehicles, posts, quotes, bookings, and messages.

I'm not getting push notifications

Check Settings → Notifications → AutoBud and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If it's already on, try signing out and back in to refresh your device token.

I confirmed my email but the app doesn't recognize me

Quit the app fully (swipe it away from the app switcher) and reopen. If sign-in still fails, tap "Forgot password" to reset.
